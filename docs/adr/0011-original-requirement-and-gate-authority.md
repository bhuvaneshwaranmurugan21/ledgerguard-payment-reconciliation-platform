# ADR 0011: Establish original requirement and gate authority

- Status: accepted for corrective workstream C1
- Date: 2026-09-02

## Context

The Part 1 conformance audit identified 331 unique requirements and 14 mandatory gates, but its
source artifacts did not constitute a checked-in, fail-closed authority for forward ownership,
reverse traceability, evidence disposition, and mechanically derived remaining work. Treating a
green test suite or a manually maintained checklist as completion would repeat the original
governance failure.

## Decision

Preserve the Phase 8 verdict bytes and the C0 accepted tree. Generate a canonical requirement
ledger, independent reverse index, and exact gate registry from digest-bound inputs. Require every
requirement to have non-empty rule, contract/schema, documentation, test, candidate-evidence, and
authority-evidence mappings plus an owner and correction action. Explicitly dispose evidence that
Phase 4 classified as orphaned; never invent a requirement mapping for it.

Derive implementation work from the resolution state. Formal amendments and C1-local remediation
remain pending the final C7 conformance audit and never mutate historical verdicts.

## Consequences

- The original 331 requirements and 14 gates have one deterministic repository authority.
- Forward and reverse coverage must agree exactly; zero effective orphans is machine-enforced.
- C2–C7 receive an exact 84-item remaining-work inventory.
- Part 1 remains in correction and Part 2 remains blocked.
- Any source, generated byte, ownership, disposition, gate, or derivation drift fails validation.

## Rejected alternatives

- Re-adjudicating Phase 8 in C1: violates the frozen audit boundary.
- Mapping Phase 4 orphan evidence to arbitrary requirements: creates false traceability.
- Maintaining remaining work separately: permits silent divergence from requirement state.
- Reusing active files as C0 evidence: permits later changes to rewrite the accepted checkpoint.
